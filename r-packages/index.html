R packages

Open-source R packages for studying research and for teaching and practising communication, marketing and user experience. All are MIT-licensed, developed in the open on GitHub and installable with remotes.

Research assessment and careers

  • metascience 0.1.0hub

    Catalogue and shared infrastructure for open metascience tools

    Lists the tools in the ecosystem with their maturity and release status, validates DOI, ORCID iD and ROR identifiers, harmonises result statuses so an unverifiable result is never read as a negative one, and writes provenance manifests.

    WebsiteGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/metascience")

  • ORMetrics 0.1.0

    Transparent open science indicators for responsible research assessment

    The R side of OpenResearchMetrics: open science indicators for a researcher from OpenAlex, DataCite and other open sources. Each indicator stands alone with its evidence and limits; there is no composite score.

    WebsiteWeb toolGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/openresearchmetrics", subdir = "r")

  • narrativecv 0.1.0

    Map narrative CV sections to evidence in open research infrastructures

    Applies an open, versioned crosswalk between narrative CV frameworks (R4RI, CVA) and ORCID, OpenAlex, DataCite and OpenAIRE, reporting section by section what evidence exists and what only the narrative can document.

    WebsiteGitHubDOI

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/narrative-cv-crosswalk", subdir = "r")

  • academiccareer 0.1.0

    Descriptive, evidence-based profiles of academic careers

    Timelines, contributions by period and career transitions across outputs, software, data, teaching, transfer and service, from ORCID, OpenAlex and DataCite. No rankings, scores or journal-based metrics.

    WebsiteExample reportGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/academiccareer")

Research integrity, reproducibility and AI use

  • reproducibility 0.1.0

    Audit the reproducibility of research projects and articles

    A checklist audit of project directories (documentation, licence, dependencies, containers, data, seeds) and transparency detectors for article full texts, with HTML and JSON reports, project templates and a GitHub Action.

    WebsiteGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/reproducibility")

  • reproimage 0.1.0

    Integrity, provenance and reproducibility checks for scientific images

    Finds identical and visually similar images, including rotated, flipped and re-contrasted copies, and records RO-Crate provenance. Shares a written specification and test images with the Python version, so both give identical results.

    WebsiteGitHubDOI

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/reproimage", subdir = "r")

  • FoSci 0.1.0

    Forensic scientometrics: integrity signals in publication data

    Retraction rates, citation stacking, template titles, tortured phrases and unusual productivity, plus paper mill screening. Every signal carries an evidence level and alternative explanations; it is never presented as proof, and people are not ranked.

    WebsiteGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/FoSci")

  • AIRes 0.1.0

    Declared use of artificial intelligence in research publications and policies

    Detects AI-use disclosure statements in articles and codes journal and institutional AI policies on a documented codebook. It measures declared use only and never tries to infer AI authorship from text.

    WebsiteGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/AIRes")

  • aitrail 0.1.0

    Document, verify and disclose the use of AI in research

    A version-controlled log of AI use by project phase, with human verification, audits of oversight gaps, reference checks against Crossref and DataCite, and disclosure statements in English and Spanish.

    WebsiteExample reportGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/aitrail")

Science communication and visual archives

  • scicomm 0.1.0

    Assess fidelity and audience fit in science communication

    Checks each version of a finding written for specialists, students and the public against the evidence: causal language, overstated certainty, lost limitations, plus clarity and readability indicators in English and Spanish.

    WebsiteExample reportGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/scicomm")

  • photoarchive 0.2.0

    Photographs across digital archives: access, harmonisation and analysis

    Brings photographs from IIIF, OAI-PMH, museum APIs, Wikidata, web pages and embedded IPTC/XMP into one schema, and analyses collections, quality, composition, saliency, colour, tone and texts in memory. Exports to CSV with a data dictionary.

    WebsiteExample reportGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/photoarchive")

Digital marketing and consumer research

  • abfunnel 0.1.0

    Design, check and analyse digital marketing experiments and funnels

    Sample size and duration planning, pre-registration templates, sample ratio mismatch checks, and effects with confidence intervals for A/B/n tests and every step of a conversion funnel.

    WebsiteExample reportGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/abfunnel")

  • visualbrief 0.1.0

    Turn campaign data into clear, reusable visual reports

    Standardises exports from ad platforms, social media and web analytics, compares content across audiences with confidence intervals, and produces accessible branded charts and a self-contained HTML report.

    WebsiteExample reportGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/visualbrief")

  • neuroconsumer 0.1.0

    Controlled visual experimentation in consumer research

    Builds visual stimuli for advertising and consumer experiments from declared factors, verifies that conditions differ only where intended, and renders deterministic SVG/PNG with Areas of Interest for eye tracking.

    WebsiteExampleGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/neuroconsumer")

UX and interaction design education

  • uxlearn 0.1.0

    Tools for learning user experience

    Usability metrics (task success, time, errors, System Usability Scale), simulated usability studies with reproducible synthetic data, case studies and a workbook for UX courses.

    WebsiteGitHub

    remotes::install_github("pablosancheznunez/uxlearn")

    IxD Lab: a learning and experimentation environment for interaction design

    Fifteen hands-on labs in three modules (Discover, Design, Evaluate) with quizzes and an optional AI tutor. Also runs in the browser with no installation.

    Open in browserGitHub
