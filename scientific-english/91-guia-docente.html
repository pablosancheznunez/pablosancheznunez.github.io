Appendix B — Teacher’s guide

The book can be used as the textbook of a doctoral workshop of about 18-24 hours (six sessions) on scientific English, or for self-study. It assumes an intermediate level of English (around B2) and focuses on research genres rather than general English.

Suggested assessment

Activity Suggested weight
Assignments (choose 4 of 6) 60%
Final task: abstract, cover letter and five-minute talk on the student’s own research 30%
Self-assessments and participation 10%

The common rubric values structure and argument (25%), clarity and concision (25%), accuracy of language (20%), appropriate academic style (20%) and reflection (10%).

Labs and activities

Labs: readability checker, hedge and booster highlighter, and Spanglish detector. Scenario: a difficult reviewer. In the PDF and EPUB, the activities appear on paper with their solutions.

Recommendations

  • Work with the students’ own texts as early as possible.
  • Normalise the difficulty: most researchers are non-native speakers (Amano et al., 2023), and clarity matters more than accent.
  • Discuss the responsible use of language tools and AI assistants, and the journals’ disclosure policies.
