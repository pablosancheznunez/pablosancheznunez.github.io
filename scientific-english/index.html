Preface

English is the main language of international research. For most researchers in the world it is a second (or third) language, and writing, reviewing and presenting in it takes extra time and effort. Amano et al. (2023) surveyed researchers in environmental sciences from many countries and found that non-native speakers spend substantially more time reading, writing and preparing presentations in English, and are more likely to have papers rejected or sent back for revision because of the language. This is a structural inequality of the research system, not a personal failing. This book cannot remove it, but it can make the work faster and the results clearer.

It is written for doctoral students and researchers in any field, with particular attention to the difficulties of Spanish speakers. It does not teach general English: it focuses on the genres researchers need (articles, abstracts, reviews, cover letters, emails, talks) and on the evidence about what makes scientific texts clear.

TipA note on AI tools

Language tools and AI assistants can help you edit, but they can also introduce errors, change your meaning or invent references. Use them as an editor, not as an author: check every change, keep your own voice, and follow the journal’s policy on disclosing their use.
