Pablo Sánchez-Núñez, PhD

Assistant Professor · Library and Information Science Department, Universidad Carlos III de Madrid

I'm interested in how people interact with information, products and services: how they behave, what captures their attention, how they make decisions, and why some experiences are more useful and meaningful than others. I'm particularly curious about how information is designed, communicated and experienced, and how digital products and services can better respond to people's needs.

I'm equally interested in how science works and evolves: how research ecosystems are built, how researchers and institutions connect, and how knowledge is produced, communicated and evaluated. I enjoy exploring the structures, relationships and practices that shape scientific activity, and how scientific systems can become more open, transparent and interconnected.

Outside academia, I love travelling, meeting interesting people and learning something new every day. I make New Year's resolutions I never keep, always go out of my way for a good curry, and disappear into nature whenever I can.
