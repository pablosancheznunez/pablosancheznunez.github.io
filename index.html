About

I'm interested in how people behave with digital products and services — and how we can make them better.

Outside work, I love travelling, meeting interesting people, and learning something new every day. If you want to know me a little better: I make New Year's resolutions I never keep, always go out of my way for a good curry, and disappear into nature whenever I get the chance.

You can find my academic profile on ORCID, my publications on Google Scholar, and more of my work on GitHub.
