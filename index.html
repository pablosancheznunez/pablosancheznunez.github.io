Pablo Sánchez-Núñez, PhD

Assistant Professor · OpenScienceLab, Library and Information Science Department, Universidad Carlos III de Madrid

I'm interested in how people interact with products and services: how they behave, what catches their attention, how they make decisions, and why some experiences are more useful than others. I'm particularly curious about how this can help us design products and services that respond to what people actually need.

I'm equally interested in how science works and evolves: how research ecosystems are built, how researchers and institutions connect, and how knowledge is produced, shared and evaluated. I enjoy exploring what shapes research, and how scientific systems can become more open and connected.

Outside academia, I love travelling, meeting interesting people and learning something new every day. I make New Year's resolutions I never keep, always go out of my way for a good curry, and disappear into nature whenever I can.
